Cognitive Shuffler is a quiet, offline sleep app. It speaks a slow stream of ordinary, concrete nouns — one small scene at a time — while an optional bed of pink or brown noise plays underneath.

There is nothing to learn, count or achieve. Let each word appear as a simple picture, then let it go.

• English and Spanish word pools, generated from open lexical data
• Phone text-to-speech, with a voice speed control
• Procedural pink/brown noise with a warmth slider
• Screen-off playback with media controls
• Sleep timer, gentle fade-out and +15 minutes from the notification
• Material 3 expressive design with wallpaper colour
• No accounts, no analytics, no internet permission

Put the phone down. The words keep going.
