## Bundled cue-word data

`assets/words/en.json` and `assets/words/es.json` are generated selections and
are released under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**.

They were derived from the following resources. Only the selected word strings,
categories and frequency bands are distributed; the upstream databases and
rating files are not redistributed.

### Princeton WordNet 3.0

- https://wordnet.princeton.edu/
- Princeton WordNet License (permissive, attribution required).

### FrequencyWords

- https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
- Repository license: MIT. The 2018 lists are derived from the OpenSubtitles
  2018 corpus (OPUS).

### MultiWordNet / Spanish WordNet

- https://github.com/wmshort/multiwordnet
- Package license: CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Spanish synsets and lemmas are used to connect Spanish words to WordNet
  concepts.

### Brysbaert et al. (2014) concreteness ratings

- Brysbaert, Warriner & Kuperman, *Concreteness ratings for 40 thousand
  generally known English word lemmas*, Behavior Research Methods.
- Used as a build-time filter for English concreteness and dominant part of
  speech. Distributed mirror: `StephanAkkerman/concreteness-ratings` on
  Hugging Face (MIT metadata).

### MEGAHit concreteness and imageability lexicon

- Ljubešić, N. (2018), *Concreteness and imageability lexicon MEGA.HR-Crossling*,
  CLARIN.SI, http://hdl.handle.net/11356/1187.
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Used as a build-time filter for Spanish concreteness and imageability.

### NLTK corpora

- Brown Corpus and CESS-ESP are used only at build time to estimate noun usage.
- NLTK itself is Apache 2.0.
